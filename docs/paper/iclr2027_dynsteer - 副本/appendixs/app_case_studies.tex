    \item \textbf{Native Default Evaluation:} The agent was allowed to run until the hard environment timeout of 100 turns, consuming 184.9 seconds of wall-clock time and over 32,000 tokens, only to fail at the end with a score of 0.0.
    \item \textbf{DynSTEER Online Behavior:} DynSTEER's frontier monitor tracked that milestone $M_2$ (\texttt{retrieve\_target\_message}) remained unsettled across the ready frontier $\mathcal{F}_t$. At Step 18, representing $18.0\%$ of trajectory progress, DynSTEER triggered a \textbf{Frontier Deadlock Stop}, terminating execution immediately. This saved \textbf{82.0\% of wasteful execution turns} and 184.9 seconds of pipeline runtime.
\end{itemize}
